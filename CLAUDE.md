# Email Studio

This studio plans and writes email campaigns for a small business. Our sample client is Kettlebird Tea, a small online tea shop launching a monthly tea box called The Steep Club.

## Folders

- `brand/`: how the brand sounds (voice guide).
- `leads/`: practice customer lists. Made-up people only.
- `campaigns/`: one folder per campaign, with its plan and emails.
- `notes/`: research notes and working notes.

## Rules

- Write in plain English. Short sentences, everyday words.
- Never send email to real people.
- Ask me before deleting anything.
