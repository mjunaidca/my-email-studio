# Kettlebird Tea · Brand voice

Kettlebird Tea is a small online tea shop. We are launching The Steep Club, a monthly tea box, to the people on our waitlist.

## Our voice in three words

**Warm. Curious. Unhurried.**

We sound like a friend who runs a little tea shop: glad you came in, happy to tell you about a new leaf, never pushy. We invite people to slow down for a cup.

## Words we use

- cup, brew, steep, leaf, kettle
- ritual, pause, slow morning, a quiet moment
- discover, try, taste, explore
- small-batch, hand-picked, from the farm
- "you", "we", "our" (talk to one person, like a letter)
- simple time words: "this month", "each month", "when you're ready"

## Words we avoid

- Hype and pressure: "last chance", "hurry", "don't miss out", "act now", "limited time", "FOMO"
- Shouting: ALL CAPS, more than one exclamation mark, strings of emoji
- Empty marketing words: "revolutionary", "game-changing", "best-ever", "world's finest", "unbeatable"
- Fussy tea snobbery: "connoisseur-grade", "for the true tea elite"
- Wellness buzzwords: "detox", "cleanse", "superfood", "boost"

## Claims we must never make

- **No health or medical claims.** Tea does not detox, cleanse, cure, heal, burn fat, help you lose weight, boost immunity or treat any condition. Never say or hint that it does.
- **No made-up numbers or urgency.** No fake countdowns, fake "only 10 boxes left", or fake discounts.
- **No promises we can't keep.** Don't promise next-day delivery, "we read every reply", or anything the team hasn't confirmed.
- **No prices or offers** unless the team has confirmed them in writing.
- **No "organic" or "fair trade"** unless the team has confirmed that specific tea is certified.

## How we sign off

End every email with:

> Steep well,
> The Kettlebird team

Keep it exactly like that: "Steep well," on one line, "The Kettlebird team" on the next.
