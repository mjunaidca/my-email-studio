# Campaign plan · The Steep Club launch

Made with `/marketing:campaign-plan`. Voice and claims follow `brand/voice.md`.

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Campaign name:** Your Monthly Pause
- **Summary:** Invite our waitlist to join The Steep Club, a monthly box of small-batch teas, with four friendly emails over four weeks.
- **Primary goal:** **300 waitlist members start a Steep Club subscription by Wednesday 6 January 2027** (the first month after launch on Monday 7 December 2026).
- **Secondary goals:**
  - Keep unsubscribes under 1% per email.
  - Get at least 50 replies or questions we can learn from.

## 2. Target audience

About 2,000 people who joined the Kettlebird Tea waitlist. They already like us enough to sign up, so they are at the **consideration** stage: interested, not yet decided.

| Group | Who they are | What they want | What might stop them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **tea-curious** | New to loose-leaf tea. Mostly drink tea bags today. | To try new teas without feeling lost. Simple brewing help. | Fear of not knowing what to do with loose leaf. |
| **gift-buyers** | Want a thoughtful present for someone who loves tea. | A gift that feels personal and arrives on time. | Not sure the person will like it; worried about timing. |
| **daily-drinkers** | Already drink tea every day. Know what they like. | Variety and good quality for their daily cup. | Already have a favourite shop; don't want waste. |

## 3. Key messages

- **Core message:** Each month, The Steep Club brings a few new small-batch teas to your door, so you always have a reason to pause for a good cup.
- **Supporting messages:**
  1. **Discover without the guesswork.** Every box comes with a simple card on how to brew each tea. (For tea-curious.)
  2. **A gift that keeps arriving.** One sign-up, a new box each month. (For gift-buyers.)
  3. **Variety for your daily ritual.** New leaves to explore alongside your usual favourites. (For daily-drinkers.)
  4. **You stay in control.** Skip a month or stop when you like. *(Team to confirm before we say this; see Risks.)*
- **Proof points:** the teas themselves (named in each box), notes from the farms we buy from, and early feedback from the sampler box. No made-up numbers.

## 4. Channel strategy

| Channel | Why it fits | Format | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Email (only channel)** | The waitlist gave us their email and asked to hear from us. It's owned, low cost and personal. | 4 short plain emails, one main button each, linking to the Steep Club page. | Medium |

Budget is small, so: **no paid ads**. Social posts can echo the emails later if there's time, but they are not part of this plan.

## 5. Content calendar

Email 01 goes to the whole waitlist. Emails 02 to 04 go to waitlist members who haven't joined yet, so new members aren't asked to join again. Each email leans towards one group, and later each group gets its own version (step 8, `/personalize`).

| # | Send date | Day | Audience group | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | 7 December 2026 | Monday | All groups (focus: everyone) | **Announce.** The Steep Club is open. What it is and how to join. |
| 02 | 10 December 2026 | Thursday | All groups (focus: tea-curious) | **Show what's inside.** This month's teas and the brewing card. |
| 03 | 15 December 2026 | Tuesday | All groups (focus: gift-buyers) | **Gift idea.** Joining for someone else, in good time for the holidays. |
| 04 | 29 December 2026 | Tuesday | All groups (focus: daily-drinkers) | **Gentle reminder.** A calm note for anyone who hasn't joined yet, before the first month ends. |

Dependencies:
- The Steep Club page (https://kettlebird.example/steep-club) must be live before email 01.
- Box contents must be final before email 02 is written.
- Gift shipping dates must be confirmed before email 03.

## 7. Success metrics

- **Primary KPI:** subscriptions started from these emails. **Target: 300 by 6 January 2027.**
- **Secondary KPIs:**
  - Open rate per email (aim for 40% or more; the list asked to hear from us)
  - Click rate on the main button (aim for 5% or more)
  - Unsubscribe rate (under 1% per email)
  - Replies and questions (50 or more)
- **Tracking:** sign-ups tagged by email on the Steep Club page; email stats from the sending service.
- **Reporting:** a short check after each send, and a one-page summary on 7 January 2027.

## 8. Budget allocation

Small budget, email only.

- Production: our own time to write and review four emails.
- Distribution: the email service we already use.
- Paid media: none.
- Contingency: keep a little time free in week 4 to resend to non-openers if results are low.

## 9. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Holiday inboxes are busy, so emails get missed. | Keep subjects short and clear. Send email 03 early enough for gifts. Don't send between 24 and 26 December. |
| We promise something the team can't deliver (for example "skip any month", delivery dates). | Only say what the team has confirmed. Check every promise before sending. |
| Sounding pushy in the last email. | No "last chance" or countdowns. A calm reminder only (see `brand/voice.md`). |
